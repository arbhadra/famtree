FAMILY TREE — THANKS & CREDITS

Everything this project drew on.

RESEARCH & INSPIRATION
----------------------
Writing and conversations that shaped which charts exist and how complex families are handled.

* Types of Family Trees – A Guide to Family Charts and Templates
  Yashodhara Keerthisena, Creately (updated 16 October 2026)
  The basis for the chart views: traditional/ancestral, descendant, vertical, horizontal, fan and extended family trees.
  https://creately.com

* Drawing a Family Map: An Experiential Tool for Engaging Children in Family Therapy
  Raymonde H. Dumont — Journal of Family Therapy 30(3), 247–259, 2008
  Cited by the Creately guide.
  https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6427.2008.00429.x

* Pruning the Family Tree: An Unexplored Path to Family Business Continuity and Family Harmony
  Johan Lambrecht & Jozef Lievens — Family Business Review 21(4), 295–313, 2008
  Cited by the Creately guide.
  https://doi.org/10.1177/08944865080210040103

* “Family tree software for complex families”
  r/Genealogy on Reddit, and everyone who replied
  Divorces, remarriages, adoptions, step-grandparents and grandparents’ siblings that other tools hide — the reason the map shows everyone. Tools suggested there (Family Historian, GenoPro, Family Echo, MacFamilyTree, MyHeritage Family Tree Builder, Family Tree Maker, Lucidchart, Creately) informed the feature set.
  https://www.reddit.com/r/Genealogy/comments/1hvrqig/

* Example family charts
  Supplied by the project owner
  A boxed descendant tree, a horizontal ancestor chart, a fan chart and a colour-coded generational tree — the visual references for the chart templates.

ALGORITHMS & STANDARDS
----------------------

* Methods for Visual Understanding of Hierarchical System Structures
  K. Sugiyama, S. Tagawa & M. Toda — IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics 11(2), 1981
  Layered drawing and the barycenter heuristic behind the family map’s row ordering.
  https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMC.1981.4308636

* An Empirical Distribution Function for Sampling with Incomplete Information
  M. Ayer, H. D. Brunk, G. M. Ewing, W. T. Reid & E. Silverman — Annals of Mathematical Statistics 26(4), 1955
  The pool-adjacent-violators algorithm, used to space each row without overlaps.
  https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177728423

* Tidier Drawings of Trees
  E. M. Reingold & J. S. Tilford — IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering SE-7(2), 1981
  The idea of centring parents over their subtrees in the descendant chart.
  https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.1981.234519

* The GEDCOM Standard, Release 5.5.1
  The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, 1999
  Genealogy interchange format used for export (including PEDI, MARR and DIV).
  https://gedcom.io

* RFC 4180: Common Format and MIME Type for CSV Files
  Y. Shafranovich, 2005
  The CSV export/import format.
  https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4180
